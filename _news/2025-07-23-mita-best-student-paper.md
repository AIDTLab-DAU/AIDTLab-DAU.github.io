Researcher **Young-Hoon Jo** won the **Best Student Paper Award** at the 21st International Conference on Multimedia Information Technology and Applications (MITA 2025) in Jeju for his research on a *Point Cloud Preprocessing 3D Gaussian Splatting Initialization Method*. The work was recognized for its innovative approach and its contribution to the field.
