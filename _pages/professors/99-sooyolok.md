# Research Experience

* 2022 – Present: Director, AI-Digital Twin-SW Demonstration Center, Dong-A University
* 2019 – Present: Full Professor, Dept. of Computer Engineering, Dong-A University, Busan, South Korea
* 2018 – 2021: Expert committee member, Busan Eco Delta Smart City National Model City Development Project (Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport)
* 2016 – 2019: Director, Offshore Plant O&M Engineering Simulation Center (Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy)
* 2013 – 2019: Director, Center for Fusion and Application of Super-computing for GPU-Based Accelerated Computing, Tongmyong University
* 2004 – 2019: Assistant, Associate and Full Professor, Dept. of Game Engineering, Junior College of Information and Communication, Tongmyong University, Busan, South Korea
* 2001 – 2003: Senior Researcher, National Institute of Information and Communications Technology (NICT), Japan

# Education

* 2001: Ph.D., Institute of Information Sciences and Electronics, University of Tsukuba, Japan
* 1998: M.S., Institute of Information Sciences and Electronics, University of Tsukuba, Japan
* 1994: B.S., Industrial Engineering, Dong-A University, Busan, South Korea

# Research Interests

* Digital twins
* GPU-based supercomputing
* Multiple-inference artificial intelligence
